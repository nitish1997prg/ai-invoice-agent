COMPANY INVOICE PROCESSING POLICY

Invoice Approval Threshold

Invoices with a total amount greater than ₹200,000 require manager approval.
Invoices at or below ₹200,000 may be approved automatically if all required
validation checks pass.

Purchase Order Requirement

Every invoice should reference a purchase order.
The purchase order must exist in the purchase order system and must have
an approved status.

Vendor Verification

The vendor issuing the invoice must exist in the vendor database.
The vendor must have an active status.

Invoice and Purchase Order Matching

The invoice vendor must match the vendor recorded on the purchase order.
Invoice items should match the purchase order items.
Invoice quantities must not exceed the quantities approved in the purchase order.
Invoice unit prices must match the purchase order unit prices.

Payment Terms

Invoice payment terms should match the payment terms registered for the vendor.
For example, if the vendor is registered with Net 30 terms, the invoice should
normally use Net 30 terms.

Discrepancy Handling

Any mismatch involving the vendor, purchase order, quantity, unit price,
payment terms, invoice total, or required documentation should be sent
for human review.

Human Review

Human reviewers may approve or reject an invoice after examining the invoice,
validation results, vendor information, purchase order information, and
relevant company policies.
